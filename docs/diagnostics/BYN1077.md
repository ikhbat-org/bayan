# BYN1077: banner comment not filled to column 80

A style lint (`sections.banner`): reported by `bayan check` only, never by `build`, `run` or `test`. Its severity comes from `lints` in `package.byn` (`.suggestion` by default; `.none` silences it, `.error` makes `bayan check` fail). `bayan fix` applies its fix whatever the severity.

**What.** A section banner (`// ── Title ─…`, alone on its line) whose `─` fill ends before or after column 80.

**Why.** Banners that end at the same column read as one ruler down the file; a ragged edge draws the eye for no reason.

**Example.**

```
// ── Parsing ──────
```

**Fix.** Lengthen or shorten the fill so the line ends at column 80 (the machine fix does it). A banner with no fill after the title, or one that would keep fewer than two `─`, is left alone.
